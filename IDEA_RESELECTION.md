# Idea Reselection

PlanRace was selected on 2026-08-31 through a zero-base process that excluded the user's history, founder-market fit, existing QECForge code, repository maturity, and sunk cost.

The full immutable evidence remains in the archived historical repository:

- [42 commodity-first candidates](https://github.com/dorakingx/qecforge-subnet/blob/main/IDEA_CANDIDATES.md)
- [Hard gates and blind official-criteria scorecard](https://github.com/dorakingx/qecforge-subnet/blob/main/IDEA_SCORECARD.md)
- [Weight sensitivity and 100,000-sample analysis](https://github.com/dorakingx/qecforge-subnet/blob/main/IDEA_SENSITIVITY.md)
- [Independent-process red team of the initial top eight](https://github.com/dorakingx/qecforge-subnet/blob/main/RED_TEAM_TOP8.md)
- [Complete current landscape snapshot](https://github.com/dorakingx/qecforge-subnet/blob/main/LANDSCAPE_CURRENT.md)

Top-three isolated spikes were PlanCache (renamed PlanRace), ScopeGuard, and ByteTwin. PlanRace passed its exactness/performance stop condition; ScopeGuard retained material Safeguard adjacency and coverage risk; ByteTwin failed because its equivalence oracle remained finite-domain/toy-only.
