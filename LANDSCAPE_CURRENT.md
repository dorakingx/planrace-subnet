## White space

No reviewed entry centered on continuously traded, executable SQL optimization
artifacts whose semantics are hard-gated on hidden generated databases before
validator-side performance scoring. Buyers are database teams, engine vendors,
and data platforms; demand repeats as distributions, queries, and releases
change. QECForge overlaps only at the generic “optimization with verification”
level and serves a much narrower quantum-circuit market.
